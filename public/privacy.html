Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

What we store

  • For each recap: its title, the award list, the reveal date and the names typed in by the group.
  • For each person: the name they chose, their votes, their 1–10 rating and the word, quote and moment they typed.
  • A random code your browser makes for each recap, stored as a one-way hash, so the site knows which name is you. No account, email, phone number or contact list.

Who sees what

  • Anyone with the recap link sees the title, the names on the list and how many have answered.
  • After the drop, anyone with the link sees the story: vote totals per award, the average rating, and the words, quotes and moments without names.
  • Nobody, including whoever started the recap, can see who voted for whom. We do not show it anywhere.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no tracking scripts, no cookies, no selling or sharing of data.
  • We never ask for or read a chat export.

Deletion

Every recap and all its answers are deleted automatically 180 days after it was made. The person who started it can delete it any time from the recap page. To ask for deletion otherwise, open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/group-wrapped with the recap link (not your personal link).

Where it runs

Crewcap runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare processes IP addresses to deliver the site; we use them only for short-lived rate limits (deleted within 2 hours) and do not log them.
