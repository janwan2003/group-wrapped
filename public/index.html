Questions

Does anyone need to sign up or install an app?

No. The link opens in any phone browser, including the one inside WhatsApp, Instagram or iMessage. You tap your name and answer. Your phone remembers you for that recap.

Can people see who voted for them?

No. The story only shows totals (“5 of 8 votes”). Quotes, words and moments are shown without names. Nobody, including the person who started it, can see who voted for whom.

Do you read our group chat?

No. Crewcap never asks for a chat export. The story is made only from what each of you types in.

When does the story drop?

Either the moment everyone on the list has answered, or on the reveal day you pick. The person who started it can also drop it early once 2 people have answered.

What if I open it on another phone?

After you answer you get a personal link. Open it on your other phone or laptop and you’re still you.

How long is it kept?

Each recap is deleted automatically 180 days after it was made. The person who started it can delete it any time.

Is it free?

Yes, with no ads and no sign-up.
