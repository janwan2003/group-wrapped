About Crewcap

Crewcap makes a year-in-review story for a group, made by the group: everyone votes the awards and adds the quote, word and best moment of the year, and the story drops for all of them at once.

It is free, has no ads and needs no account or app. Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/group-wrapped.

Crewcap is not affiliated with Spotify or any other “year in review” product.
